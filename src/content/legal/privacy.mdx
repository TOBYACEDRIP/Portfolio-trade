This policy explains how **TheTobyAce** ("we", "us"), based in Nigeria, handles personal information when you visit **thetobyace.com**, contact us, or enrol in a Programme. We collect as little as possible.

## 1. What this website collects

**This website has no user accounts, does not store forms and does not use advertising cookies.**

- **Anonymous analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand which pages are visited and how fast they load. These tools are designed to work **without cookies** and report aggregated statistics, not individual profiles.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider (Vercel) may briefly process technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the website securely.
- **Fonts and images** are served from our own website, so loading a page does not send your data to font providers.

## 2. What you choose to send us

The private mentorship **application form does not store anything on this website**. When you press "Apply on Telegram" or "Apply by email", your answers are copied or placed into a message that **you** send to us through Telegram or your email app.

When you contact us or enrol, we may receive:

- your name and contact details (email address, Telegram username);
- what you tell us about your trading experience, goals and challenges;
- messages, charts or trading journals you share during a Programme;
- payment confirmations (we do not receive or store your full card details — payments are handled by the payment provider).

## 3. How we use your information

We use it only to:

- reply to your enquiry or application and decide whether a Programme is a good fit;
- deliver the Programme — scheduling, sessions, chart reviews, feedback and community access;
- handle payments, refunds and record-keeping;
- keep our community safe and enforce our [Terms of Service](/terms).

We do **not** sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to run the website and Programmes, for example:

- **Vercel** — website hosting and analytics;
- **Zoom** — live classes and private sessions;
- **Telegram** — messaging and the student community;
- **Email provider** (Google Gmail) — correspondence;
- **Payment provider** — processing payments and refunds.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. Some of them may process data outside your country.

## 5. Recordings

Group classes may be recorded and shared **only with enrolled students**. You can keep your camera off and use a display name. Private sessions are only recorded where the programme includes recordings, and those recordings are shared only with you.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries that don't lead to enrolment for up to **12 months**, and student records for as long as needed to deliver the Programme and meet legal or accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- correct it or ask us to delete it;
- object to or restrict how we use it;
- withdraw consent at any time.

To make a request, email **[thetobyace@gmail.com](mailto:thetobyace@gmail.com)**. You may also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)**, or to the data protection authority where you live.

## 8. Children

The website and Programmes are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 9. Links to other websites

Our pages link to YouTube, Telegram, X, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. Those platforms have their own privacy practices, which apply once you leave this website.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
