Please read this disclosure carefully before acting on anything you learn from TheTobyAce.

<Callout title="Important">
Trading foreign exchange and other leveraged products carries a **high level of risk** and may not be suitable for everyone. You can lose some or all of the money you trade — and with some products, more than your initial deposit.
</Callout>

## 1. Education, not advice

All content on this website and in our Programmes is **educational**. It is not financial, investment or legal advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell any instrument. We do not know your financial situation, objectives or risk tolerance.

## 2. Leverage magnifies losses

Leverage allows you to control a large position with a small amount of capital. It magnifies gains **and losses** equally. Small price movements can lead to large losses in a short time.

## 3. Analysis is not certainty

Market analysis — including Market Narrative, directional bias, points of interest and entry models — describes **expectations, not certainties**. Markets can move against any analysis. An idea is only ever a hypothesis until price confirms it, and confirmed ideas can still fail.

## 4. Past performance

Past performance, examples, chart breakdowns and hypothetical scenarios are **not indicative of future results**. Illustrations on this website are teaching diagrams, not real trading records.

## 5. No guaranteed outcomes

No course, mentorship tier or framework can guarantee profits, consistency, passing a prop firm challenge or any other outcome. How you apply what you learn — and the results — are your responsibility.

## 6. Prop firm challenges

Proprietary trading firm challenges have their own rules, fees and risks. Passing a challenge does not guarantee payouts, and firms may change or enforce their rules at their discretion.

## 7. Trade only what you can afford to lose

Never trade with money you need for living expenses, debts or emergencies. If you are unsure whether trading is suitable for you, seek advice from an independent, licensed financial adviser.
