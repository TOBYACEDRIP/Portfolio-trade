Please read this policy before you pay. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

<Callout title="No refunds">
**All payments for The Liquidity Room and private mentorship (Standard, Premium and Elite) are final and non-refundable.** By paying, you confirm that you have read and accepted this policy.
</Callout>

## 1. All fees are non-refundable

Once payment has been made, **no refunds are given**, whether before or after the programme starts. This applies to:

- The Liquidity Room group mentorship;
- Standard 1-on-1, Premium Private and Elite Extended Coaching.

Places are limited and are reserved for you, and private programmes are prepared around you as soon as you enrol. That is why payments cannot be returned.

## 2. This includes, for example

Refunds are not given in situations such as:

- changing your mind after paying;
- missing classes or sessions;
- not using the community, recordings or materials;
- trading losses, failing a prop firm challenge, or not achieving a particular result;
- removal from a Programme for breaching our [Terms of Service](/terms).

Trading involves significant risk and no education can guarantee outcomes. See our [Risk Disclosure](/risk-disclosure).

## 3. If you can't attend

- **The Liquidity Room:** missed classes can't be repeated individually. Recordings are shared where available. If you can't continue, you may ask to move to a future group once. This is at our discretion and depends on availability.
- **Private mentorship:** you may reschedule a session with at least **24 hours' notice**. Sessions missed without 24 hours' notice count as delivered.

## 4. If we can't deliver

If **we** cancel a class or session, we will reschedule it at a time that works for you. In the unlikely event we cannot deliver sessions you have paid for, we will refund the value of the undelivered sessions only.

Nothing in this policy affects any rights you have that cannot be excluded under Nigerian law, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.

## 5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank or payment provider. Most issues can be solved quickly. Opening a chargeback for a programme you have accepted under this policy may result in loss of access to the Programme and community.

## 6. Questions

If you're unsure whether a programme is right for you, ask **before** paying: **[thetobyace@gmail.com](mailto:thetobyace@gmail.com)**, or apply and choose "Not sure — advise me".
