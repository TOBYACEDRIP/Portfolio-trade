We want every student to enrol with confidence and clear expectations. This policy explains when refunds are available for each Programme. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

<Callout title="In short">
Full refund if you cancel **before your programme starts**. Once the first class or session has taken place, fees are non-refundable — except where we are unable to deliver what you paid for.
</Callout>

## 1. The Liquidity Room (group mentorship)

- **Before the first live class:** cancel in writing and you will receive a **full refund**.
- **After the first live class has taken place:** fees are **non-refundable**, because your place, the curriculum and community access have been provided.
- **Missed classes:** we cannot refund or repeat individual classes you miss. Recordings are shared where available.
- **Moving to a later group:** if you cannot continue, you may ask to transfer to a future group once, at our discretion and subject to availability.

## 2. Private mentorship (Standard, Premium and Elite)

- **Before your first session:** cancel in writing and you will receive a **full refund**.
- **After your first session has taken place:** fees are **non-refundable**. The first session includes an individual assessment and a personalised plan prepared for you.
- **If we cannot deliver:** if we cancel sessions and cannot reschedule them within the programme, you will receive a **pro-rata refund** for the sessions not delivered.

## 3. Rescheduling private sessions

- You may reschedule a private session with at least **24 hours' notice**.
- Sessions missed without 24 hours' notice are treated as delivered.
- If we need to reschedule, we will give as much notice as possible and offer a new time.

## 4. No refunds for results

Refunds are not available because of trading losses, failing a prop firm challenge, or not achieving a particular result. Trading involves significant risk and no education can guarantee outcomes — see our [Risk Disclosure](/risk-disclosure).

## 5. Removal for misconduct

If you are removed from a Programme for breaching our [Terms of Service](/terms) — for example, sharing recordings or materials — no refund is due.

## 6. How to request a refund

Email **[thetobyace@gmail.com](mailto:thetobyace@gmail.com)** with your name, the Programme you enrolled in and the payment reference. We aim to respond within **5 working days**. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; processing times depend on your bank or payment provider, and any non-refundable fees charged by the payment provider may be deducted.

## 7. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a dispute with your bank or payment provider — most issues can be solved quickly. Opening a chargeback for a programme that has been delivered may result in loss of access to the Programme and community.
